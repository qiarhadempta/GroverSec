Research Areas

Our research spans multiple domains of cybersecurity, combining theoretical foundations with practical application


Cryptography & Post-Quantum Security

As quantum computing advances, cryptographic systems relying on classical hardness assumptions face long-term risk. This area evaluates the readiness of post-quantum signature schemes for real-world deployment, with emphasis on performance trade-offs during protocol handshakes.

  • Post-quantum signature schemes
  • Performance evaluation of quantum-resistant algorithms
  • Integration of post-quantum cryptography into existing protocols
Meet the Lead

Network Security

This area focuses on identifying and mitigating threats at the network layer, from anomaly detection in traffic patterns to uncovering protocol-level weaknesses that are often overlooked in standard security audits.

  • Anomaly-based intrusion detection for enterprise network traffic
  • Protocol-level vulnerability analysis in modern network infrastructure
  • Traffic classification techniques for early threat identification
Meet the Lead

AI & Machine Learning Security

As machine learning systems are increasingly deployed in security-critical contexts, this area examines how adversaries can manipulate model behavior, and what defenses can preserve model integrity under attack.

  • Adversarial example generation and robustness testing
  • Defense mechanisms against model evasion and poisoning attacks
  • Security implications of large-scale AI system deployment
Meet the Lead

IoT & Embedded Systems Security

Resource-constrained devices often trade security for efficiency. This emerging research area explores lightweight mechanisms that preserve device performance while closing common security gaps in embedded systems.

  • Lightweight authentication protocols for constrained hardware
  • Security risk assessment in consumer and industrial IoT devices
  • Firmware-level vulnerability identification
